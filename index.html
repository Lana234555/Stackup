Process

From idea to launch

A clear, proven process to turn your idea
into a successful digital product — without the guesswork.

  1. We discuss goals, audience, and budget, then lock in a clear project scope.

    1–2 days

  2. We create the structure, user flow and responsive UI, then turn it into a clickable prototype.

    3–7 days

  3. We build the product, connect the required integrations and make sure everything works across devices.

    1–3 weeks

  4. Final QA, deployment and handoff with everything you need to manage and grow the product.

    1–2 days
